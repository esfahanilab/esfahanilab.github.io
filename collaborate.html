Collaborate

The hardest problems in cancer are shared problems

Our methods are only as good as the questions, samples, and clinical insight they are applied to. We welcome collaborations with academic laboratories and clinical groups, partnerships with companies interested in our technologies, and philanthropic support that lets us pursue ideas before they are fundable.

Academic labs

Research collaborations

We partner with experimental, clinical, and computational groups who have a biological question that our methods can help answer, or a dataset that can test and sharpen them.

  • Cell-free DNA fragmentomics and gene expression inference (EPIC-Seq) applied to new cancer types and clinical settings
  • Minimal residual disease monitoring and dynamic risk modeling in trials and cohorts
  • Tumor microenvironment and spatial analyses
  • Co-mentoring of students and postdocs across disciplines

Industry

Technology partnerships

Companies interested in licensing, co-developing, or validating our technologies are welcome to get in touch. Several of our methods, including EPIC-Seq, CIRI, and our lymphoma classifiers, have moved from publication toward clinical application.

  • Licensing of published and unpublished methods
  • Sponsored research and validation studies
  • Joint development of assays and analysis pipelines

Licensing is handled with the Stanford Office of Technology Licensing. We are happy to make the introduction.

Philanthropy

Gifts to the lab

Philanthropic gifts let us take on high-risk ideas, support trainees, and generate the preliminary data that larger grants require. Gifts of any size make a difference, and all are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

  • Seed funding for new early-detection and MRD projects
  • Fellowships for postdoctoral scholars and students
  • Computing and sequencing resources

Gifts are made through Stanford Medicine and can be directed to the Esfahani Lab. Please contact us and we will connect you with the Stanford Medicine development team, or visit Stanford Medical Giving.
