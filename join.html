Join us

Work on the earliest signals of cancer

We are looking for people who are excited by hard inference problems with real clinical stakes. You will work with deep sequencing data from large patient cohorts, build models that have to be right, and collaborate with clinicians, biologists, and engineers across Stanford.

Open

Postdoctoral Fellow

An exceptional postdoctoral scholar to join the team and contribute to research in computational oncology, with a focus on cell-free DNA, fragmentomics, and machine learning.

Required

  • PhD in computational biology, cancer biology, bioengineering, or a related field with substantial computational experience. MDs with prior computational oncology experience are encouraged to apply.
  • Strong record of peer-reviewed publications.
  • Skilled in R and/or Python.
  • Working knowledge of statistical modeling and machine learning.
  • Understanding of high-throughput sequencing: whole genome, exome, targeted capture, RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, ATAC-seq, scATAC-seq.
  • Strong oral and written communication, and a desire to contribute to lab meetings and one-on-one discussions.

Desirable

  • Familiarity with NGS tools such as bwa, STAR, bedtools, samtools, Picard, RSEM, GISTIC, GATK, Strelka, and Seurat.
  • Experience with spatial transcriptomic data.
  • Experience modeling temporal or longitudinal data.
  • Experience with Bayesian modeling.

Students

Graduate & rotation students

Stanford PhD students interested in a rotation, and master's students in Computer Science, Statistics, ICME, or Biomedical Informatics looking for research projects, are welcome to reach out with a CV and a short note on what you would like to work on.

Analysts

Bioinformatics scientists

We periodically hire bioinformatics analysts and scientists with training in statistical modeling and experience analyzing high-throughput sequencing data. If that is you, get in touch even when no posting is open.
